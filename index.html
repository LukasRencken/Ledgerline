Open Ledgerline How it works

1 · Inbound Reconciliation

Drop receipts (or images via OCR), a bank statement, and multi-currency lines. Ledgerline matches by vendor, amount, and settlement window, then explains every suggestion in plain English.

2 · Model Integrity

Snapshot your budget model — paste JSON or drop an .xlsx. Every cell change is diffed, logged, and flagged when a material assumption moves.

3 · One Audit Trail

Both modules write to a hash-chained, checkpointed event log. Export a sealed audit package that traces any number back to its evidence.

Built on accounting principles

  • Double-entry — every accepted match posts a balanced debit and credit; reversals post the opposite.
  • Immutability — append-only log, SHA-256 hash-chained, with a separate checkpoint to detect truncation.
  • Materiality — anomaly thresholds configurable per absolute and relative magnitude.
  • Local-first — your ledger never leaves this browser tab. Persisted locally, exportable anytime.
